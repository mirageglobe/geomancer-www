1. Mount Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, East Malaysia. 马来西亚, 沙巴州, 神山.

重阳节登高新罗经开光吉祥物祈福于神山

I came to Mount Kota Kinabalu (Mount KK) about 20 years ago. It was just a visit to fulfill my curiosity about this mountain. Since then, I went back there as often as 4 to 5 times a year… Mount Kota Kinabalu is a charger to refresh physical and mental energies … It is really good!
》第一次到访神山约在20年前，当时，我只是带着对这座神山的好奇心态…我无法形容我的感受，从此，我每年都会回到神山来…


Mount Kota Kinabalu … It is really good! …

There are many resorts with different type of feelings around the mid hill of Mount KK, some face one side of the mountain, whereas others may face the other sides of the mountain, but all are just as good. The weather is cool, sometimes may be a little cold. I have never tried to go to the top of Mount KK nor have I ever intended to climb it (not fond of height!), but from the many stories that I have heard about the challenging journey to the top. I thought I would rather relax myself in the cool breeze at the resorts…

Apart from the morning TaiJi exercise, enjoying the cool breeze in the bright warm sun and fortune telling are some of the activities. Plans to conduct cultural talks, seminars and fengshui courses are among some of the future visits …

2 – The Ranau Estate near Kota Kinabaru… 神山附近的一处“仙境”…
“人间仙境 A dream house at Ranau, near Mount KK Sabah, Malaysia” …
Far away from the city, from the crowds, into the nature, into heaven …
这里没有胡言乱语， 这里什么都没有，只有新鲜空气和鸟语…

Someone told me that this is an “excluded site”, situated somewhere near the foot hill of mount Kota Kinabalu. I visited the site few years ago in the hope of locating a place for Tao culture practices, sadly, this was what I saw…
》曾有人告诉我，神山附近有个“仙境”… 很·兴奋的来到这里… 一望无际，真是太好了！… It was good when you look far and high…


然而，当我访问一户人家… 真是晴天霹雳！…
However, when I went into one of the houses …its a nightmare!…

人们就是不懂得爱惜好的环境…
There are good places, but just too few people to appreciate them …

3 – 沙巴州金岭树上屋沙巴金岭树上屋
The Golden Peak Resort (Tree-Top Houses)

This is an interesting resort. Each room is built from the support of a large tree. A large tree trunk can be seen in the middle of the room separating the beds. The temperature at night is about 15 degree, not bad, all you need is a long sleeve sweater to feel quite comfortable. The toilet is good, you do not feel the jungle once the toilet door is shut and there is hot water for a good wash before going into total darkness at mid-night!
An experience of a night in the jungle probably like “Tarzan – the Jungle Man”.
All electrical lights were shut down at mid-night and remained in total darkness through out the night until dawn. There is of course a danger of colliding with the tree trunk at the middle of the room if one tries to move about in total darkness in the room. In darkness, there is only the continuous symphonic orchestra music rehearsing over and over by many insects until day break! Go to bed early would be the best idea! Do not forget that this is a jungle!

These stones pillars were erected and used in the Middle Neolithic Age between 2550 and 1600BC.
