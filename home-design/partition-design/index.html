Partition Design屏风设计

（林质彬的设计 CPLim’s design）
There are many ways to put up a divider to separate a large space. Divider is usually known as Partition. Some are built for decoration, some are to create privacy or to block uncomfortable reflections. This page consists of same examples of partition design.

The purpose of the partition is to redirect straight and long travel external energies entering a building, the partition will bend, turn or slowdown these energies enter from inauspicious direction to a moving flow creating alternative auspicious energy flows.
